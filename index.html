How to install

  1. Tap the blue button above.
  2. When the download finishes, tap it to open.
  3. If your phone asks, tap Settings and turn on Allow from this source, then go back and tap Install.
  4. Open Pocket Brain and tap Get my brain. Use Wi-Fi: it's a one-time 2.6 GB download.

Will it work on my phone?

Android phones with 8 GB of memory or more.

Most phones from the last 4 to 5 years. iPhone is not supported yet.

Updates

The app checks for new versions on its own and shows an "Update ready" button. Tap it and install, same as above.
